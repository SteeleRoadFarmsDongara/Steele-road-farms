Old orders — already delivered

Orders from on or before Fri 13 Nov 2026 were already delivered — they do not count for this fortnight and are not in the pack list or totals. Kept here for the record.

Wait list — for next fortnight

Orders placed after the Thursday cutoff — or once the week hits its 15-box cap — wait here. They are not in this fortnight's pack list. When you close the fortnight they roll into next fortnight's orders automatically. Or move one now with its button (it goes straight into the orders list and shows in the pack list).

When the wait list builds up and there is enough stock, bring wait-listed orders forward to tomorrow — Geraldton and Dongara customers alike. Pick them from the wait list below with Next-day delivery, then copy the message to tell each customer.

When a new customer orders their first box with someone’s referral code, the new customer gets 50% off that box and the referrer gets a FREE box the same size. Owed free boxes are listed here — tap Mark as claimed when you’ve given it to them.

Fortnightly reset & past orders

When the fortnight's deliveries and pickups are done, close the fortnight. That files all of this fortnight's orders away, clears the pack list, and starts you fresh for next fortnight. Gift cards and shop inventory are not cleared. Order numbers restart each fortnight — past fortnights are filed below. Wait-listed orders roll into the new fortnight automatically.

Shop inventory — edit

Change prices, stock and gift card amounts here. Hit Save inventory and the Shop updates straight away on this device. This saves on this device only — same as orders on GitHub Pages.

Stock count is only for you — customers never see it. The Shop only shows ✅ Available or ❌ Not available. Set the stock count to 0 and that veggie flips to Not available in the Shop straight away; put a number back in and it is Available again.
